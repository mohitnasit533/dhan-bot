Strategy rules

15-minute closed candles

All conditions must confirm: EMA 21/34 crossover, Supertrend direction, close on the correct side of VWAP, and a narrow CPR breakout (CPR width ≤ 0.5% of the prior close).

BUY signal → sell PE near −0.40 delta. SELL signal → sell CE near +0.40 delta. Use the monthly stock-option expiry through its final seven calendar days, then shift to the next month’s chain.
