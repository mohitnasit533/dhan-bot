Dhan connection

Trade controls

Manual confirmation

Off = manual preview and confirmation. On = confirmed signals open Paper trades automatically; Live always requires confirmation.

Fixed option-premium stop loss: 20%. Target: 50%. Stock options only; maximum five open trades. Entries: weekdays 09:45 to before 14:00 IST. Dhan requires a whitelisted static IP for live orders.

Strategy rules

15-minute closed candles

All conditions must confirm: EMA 21/34 crossover, Supertrend direction, close on the correct side of VWAP, and a narrow CPR breakout (CPR width ≤ 0.5% of the prior close).

BUY signal → sell PE near −0.40 delta. SELL signal → sell CE near +0.40 delta. Use the current monthly stock-option expiry; switch to next month's monthly expiry during the final seven calendar days before expiry.

Scanner universe: all NSE stock-option underlyings. Stocks must have at least ₹5 crore traded value today; qualifying names are ranked by turnover and scanned.
